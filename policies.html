Terms and policies

Everything you agree to when you book Alex Studio, in plain words.

Terms of service

Alex Studio provides photography and films for weddings, engagement and pre-wedding shoots, haldi, mehendi and sangeet, birthdays, anniversaries and parties, personal portraits and small corporate events.

A booking is confirmed once we share a written quote on WhatsApp, you accept it, and the advance stated in that quote is received. The quote lists the date, hours, team and what you receive.

Please share correct event details (date, time, venue and contact person). Changes on the day that add hours or locations may be charged as per the quote.

First booking offer: 10% off

New customers get 10% off the quoted photography fee on their first booking with Alex Studio.

The offer is valid once per customer, must be mentioned before the booking is confirmed, and cannot be combined with any other offer. It does not apply to travel, venue or third-party costs.

Payments, rescheduling and cancellation

The advance reserves your date and team. The balance is payable as written in your quote.

If you need to change the date, tell us as early as possible. We will move your booking to a new date if our team is available.

Cancellation and refund terms are written in your quote before you pay. Please read them before confirming.

Photo delivery

Edited photos are delivered online in the format and timeline written in your quote.

Please download and keep a copy of your photos. We keep backup copies for a limited time only.

Photo usage and copyright

You may use your photos for personal use, printing and sharing with family and friends.

We will only use your photos on our website or social pages with your permission.

Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to arrange your booking: your name, phone number and event details, shared by you on WhatsApp.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with our own team members working on your event.

This website may use cookies and similar tools from advertising partners such as Google to measure visits and ad performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Disclaimer

Photos shown on this website are for illustration and show the kinds of events we cover.

Final coverage, team and deliverables are as written in your quote.

Contact

Alex Studio takes all enquiries and bookings on WhatsApp. Chat with us on WhatsApp.
